One scene, eight ways to tell it

The same moment from the Giant Peach film, "It grew bigger... and BIGGER!", drawn in each storytelling style I can build well. Every sample is live code, so what you see is what a full video in that style would look like. The references are real books, films and artists to look at for each direction, not things I would copy.

"Strong fit" means I can make a full video in that style at the quality of the Giant Peach film. "Good fit" means it works well, with a limit noted under "Watch out for". Styles that depend on frame-by-frame character acting, such as anime or Disney-style cel animation, are left out on purpose, because my version would look stiff.
